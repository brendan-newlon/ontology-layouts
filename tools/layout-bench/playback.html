Time experiments not the shipped settings

The layout fills its time limit: the time a left-out step frees goes to the next variant, to more restarts and to the clean-up, so leaving a step out barely changes the total. These switches stop that refilling, so a change shows what it really saves. The result line compares against every step with the shipped settings.
