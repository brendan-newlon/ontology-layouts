Layout Lab

Drag a class to move it. Ctrl+click classes to select several, then drag one to move them together; Shift+drag on empty space selects an area. Drag on a link to pull a bend into it; drag a bend to reshape; double-click a bend to remove it, or a link to straighten it. Drag empty space to pan, wheel to zoom. Ctrl+Z undoes the last step and greys it out as a trial; "ignore" on any step leaves it out of the analysis. Coming back to a graph continues where you left it; "Lay out again" starts that graph over.
